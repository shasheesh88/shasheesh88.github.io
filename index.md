## Who I Am

I don’t rush toward conclusions. I prefer understanding. I prefer depth over speed, clarity over noise, and substance over appearance.

Much of my life is centered around learning, thinking, building, and refining — not just externally, but internally.

Over time, this space becomes more valuable, because it grows alongside me.
