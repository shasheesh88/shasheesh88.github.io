    <span class="col-label">Core Beliefs</span>
    <p>The main challenge I have faced in life journey so far is this, even when I know I am unique and capable, and I work wholeheartedly on something meaningful, I often do not get enough opportunities to show or use my skills. This makes me feel like giving up or being lost.</p>
    <p>I believe I can do extraordinary things, but I am often evaluated or screened by people who are not the right ones for that responsibility. Their approval is still required for me to join a team. That is what saddens me most.</p>
    <p>I believe good health and a supportive family are very important for producing the best version of yourself.</p>
    <p>I have developed the habit of positive self-talk. Many times the outcome is not in my favor, but this practice gives me the courage to keep moving forward in the winds of failure.</p>
    <blockquote><p>I remind myself that Shashi is calm, Shashi is peaceful, Shashi is mindful, and healthy.</p></blockquote>
    <p>Along the way, I have been fortunate to be surrounded by a good environment and good people. Sometimes I feel I am simply lucky. I am grateful for my parents, the chance to play exceptional badminton, the guidance I received for my master's thesis, my manager, joining NITK, and meeting good people there. All these experiences shaped my journey.</p>
    <p>Sometimes I also feel that, despite emotional ups and downs, the choices I made eventually placed me in environments where I felt comfortable and supported.</p>
